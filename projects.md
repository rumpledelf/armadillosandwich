These are my personal projects.

My professional work isn’t linked here — much of it has been rebuilt or replaced over time — but over the years it has included large Australian corporate and government sites, data platforms, campaign microsites, and high-traffic public information systems.

I’m currently focused on my own long-term projects and infrastructure. Some of the projects listed below are actively under development, some are stable and focussed on content production, and some are preserved as archives.

## Data Platform (Analytics & Ranking)

A data-driven ranking and analytics system built on Backdrop with custom ingestion pipelines. It aggregates and normalises large volumes of third-party data, presents a variety of structured visualisations, and allows visitors to perform their own searches and analyses.

## Photographic Dictionary

<a href="https://photographicdictionary.com">Photographic Dictionary</a> is a long-running photo dictionary illustrating common words with curated images. Originally launched in 2009, it is now powered by a custom PHP backend and continues to evolve as a structured content system.

## Words, by Cat

<a href="https://wordsbycat.com">Words, by Cat</a> is a companion site to Photographic Dictionary, where fun photos with cats in get their own showcase. Generated with a custom Python static site generator, it serves as a lightweight spinoff of the main project.

## Giraffian

<a href="https://giraffian.com">Giraffian</a> is a collection of children’s jokes, cartoons and simple activities dating back to 2002. It now runs as a no-database PHP site with light templating and pagination, and remains online as an archive of an earlier era.

## Stancartoons
<a href="https://stancartoons.com">Stancartoons</a> is a publishing platform for my father’s cartoons. Rebuilt in 2024 and hosted on WordPress.com, it supports ongoing publication of new and archived work.
